Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-08

Vault Sync is a personal integration used only by its owner. It is not a public service and has no other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent, given through Google's sign-in:

  • Google Calendar - it reads the events of the owner's calendars, and creates, updates and deletes the events that correspond to tasks in the owner's Obsidian vault.
  • Google Drive, read-only - it reads the owner's Google Docs, Sheets and Slides and converts them into notes. It never changes, moves or deletes anything in Drive.

Where the data goes

The data is used only for the owner, on the owner's own computer: in the Obsidian app and in the owner's notes, which the owner backs up to their own repository. To write those notes, the owner's own tools include an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic), which processes the content on the owner's behalf.

This app runs no server of its own. The data is not shared with anyone else, not sold, and not used for advertising or for training AI models. The sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions.
